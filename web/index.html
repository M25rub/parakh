About Parakh

Students get dozens of "opportunity" emails. Some are real, some are traps that ask for fees, documents or passwords. Checking each one by hand is slow and boring, so most people guess.

Parakh is an agent, not a single rule. It decides which checks each email needs, runs them, looks for the opportunity on the official website, and explains its verdict with evidence you can read.

Try it on an email

What Parakh checks

Every verdict is built from evidence. The agent picks the checks that matter for each email.

Sender

Reads the email headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) to see if the sender is who they claim to be.

Links

Follows where links really go, checks Google Safe Browsing, domain age and look-alike names.

QR codes

Decodes QR codes inside emails and checks the hidden link like any other.

Fees & pressure

Spots "pay to register" requests and "reply in 24 hours" pressure tactics.

Official source

Searches the real organisation's website to see if the opportunity actually exists.

Shared memory

Remembers scam fingerprints, so a trap caught once is recognised for every student.
